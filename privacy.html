Privacy

This site holds pictures for the Instagram page @sonekeechidiyaa. It has no forms, no sign-in, no cookies of its own and no tracking.

The page uses a small program to publish its posts through Instagram's own tools.

If you comment the word VERSE on one of the page's posts, the page may send you one direct message with that post's verse. To do that, the program reads the public comments on the page's own posts. It keeps a note of the Instagram username and account number of each person whose comment was that word, and whether the message went, so that nobody is messaged twice. Other comments are read and not kept. That note stays on the page owner's computer and is not shared or sold.

The message is automatic. If you reply to it, a person reads your reply.

To ask for your username to be removed from that note, send a direct message to @sonekeechidiyaa on Instagram.
